How this works

Each weekday before the market opens, the tracker pulls the latest prices, SEC filings, Reddit mentions, short-sale volume and Google Trends for the watchlist, then predicts whether each stock will close higher or lower than its last close, one trading day and one week (five trading days) ahead.

Every prediction is locked the moment it is made: the database refuses edits and deletes, each row is chained to the one before it with a hash, and the daily commit on GitHub timestamps it publicly. The scorecard compares the hit rate against two baselines that need no skill: always predicting "up", and predicting each stock will move the same way the overall market (SPY) just did.

This is an experiment, not investment advice. Small samples swing a lot, so give it a few weeks before reading much into the numbers.
